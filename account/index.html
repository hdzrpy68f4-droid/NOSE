At least 10 characters. Length matters more than symbols — a short phrase you can remember beats a short password you cannot.

Your palate

Your saved jars live in this browser, exactly as they did before accounts existed. Sync is not switched on yet — when it is, it will be something you turn on deliberately, not a default.

Use the export button in the matcher to move a palate between devices in the meantime.

Delete your account

This erases, in this order:

  • any palate synced to our storage;
  • every active session, on every device;
  • your account and email address.

It does not touch the palate saved in this browser — that stays yours until you clear it. It cannot be undone, and we keep no copy to restore from.

Anonymous match-feedback votes and error reports are not deleted, because they were never attached to you. Neither carries an account identifier. This is set out on the privacy page.
