Earthy

Damp earth, musk, clove, mossy wood and grounded plant material.

What this family can resemble

These references help translate a terpene panel into everyday scent language. They are comparisons, not literal ingredient claims.

  • Damp earth
  • Mossy bark
  • Musk
  • Clove
  • Wood

Compounds used by this model

NOSE currently groups these commonly reported compounds into the earthy family:

  • Myrcene

How it can shape the fingerprint

In the NOSE model, earthy signals often form the base of a profile. They can make brighter citrus, pine or floral notes feel deeper and more grounded.

What the family does not tell you

Earthy is a descriptive family, not a quality grade. Storage, curing and moisture can also change how grounded notes present to the nose.
