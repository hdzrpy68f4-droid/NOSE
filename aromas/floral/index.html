Floral

Lavender, rose, chamomile, soft perfume and delicate spice.

What this family can resemble

These references help translate a terpene panel into everyday scent language. They are comparisons, not literal ingredient claims.

  • Lavender
  • Rose
  • Chamomile
  • Soft perfume
  • Delicate spice

Compounds used by this model

NOSE currently groups these commonly reported compounds into the floral family:

  • Linalool
  • α-Bisabolol
  • α-Terpineol
  • Nerolidol

How it can shape the fingerprint

In the NOSE model, floral signals soften the profile and add perfumed or blossom-like character. They often appear as a secondary layer rather than the entire profile.

What the family does not tell you

Floral language is comparative and familiar, not a promise that the jar will smell like a single named flower.

A family is one part of the fingerprint.
