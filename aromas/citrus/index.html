Citrus

Bright peel, zest, grapefruit, orange and sharp pith-like aroma language.

What this family can resemble

These references help translate a terpene panel into everyday scent language. They are comparisons, not literal ingredient claims.

  • Lemon peel
  • Orange zest
  • Grapefruit
  • Bright pith
  • Fresh rind

Compounds used by this model

NOSE currently groups these commonly reported compounds into the citrus family:

  • Limonene

How it can shape the fingerprint

In the NOSE model, a citrus-heavy profile reads as bright and lifted. It can sit on top of earthy, spice, floral or herbal signals rather than appearing alone.

What the family does not tell you

Citrus is about measured aroma character. It does not mean the product will taste exactly like fruit or produce a particular effect.
